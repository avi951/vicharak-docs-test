Power Sources
=============

Power Rating
------------

.. important::

   - **Minimum Power:** 10W (12V @ 0.83A)
   - **Recommended Power:** 15W (12V @ 1.25A)

   If you are connecting all peripherals, such as Cameras, Displays, USB, HDMI, PCIe etc. the power requirement will range between **20W-25W** (up to 12V @ 2.1A). This is necessary when the board is running at full load.

The Axon Mini board is powered through the dedicated Type-C PD port using a 12V adapter.

1. 12V Power Source (Dedicated Type-C PD Port)
----------------------------------------------
You can power the board using a 12V adapter through the dedicated Type-C PD port.

- **Minimum Power:** 10W
- **Recommended Power:** 15W
- **Maximum Power:** 30W
