==========================
Axon Mini Video Interface
==========================

Axon Mini, powered by the Qualcomm Dragonwing **QCS6490**, includes MIPI CSI
camera inputs and an Adreno Video Processing Unit (VPU) for hardware-accelerated
video encode and decode.
